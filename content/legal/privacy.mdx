# Privacy Policy

> **Draft — to be reviewed by the organising team.** This page is a plain-English draft, not legal advice. Items marked [placeholder] still need a decision.

Synaxis is a science olympiad run by students of the Heuser Science Society (HSS) at Heuser College, Karachi. This policy explains what information we collect when you register a team, why we need it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

## Who we are

"We", "us" and "the Cubinet" mean the Synaxis organising council of the Heuser Science Society. For anything in this policy, contact us at the email address shown in the website footer.

## What we collect

When a Head Delegate registers a team, we collect:

- **Team members:** full name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, grade or level, and institution for every member.
- **Head Delegate:** the above, plus an alternate phone number (optional) and city.
- **Guardian or emergency contact:** name and phone number.
- **Registration details:** delegation type, team name, module preferences, how you heard about Synaxis, and any dietary or accessibility needs you choose to tell us.
- **Payment:** the fee amount, and the bank-transfer receipt you upload or send on WhatsApp.
- **Waivers:** the signed waiver documents you upload.
- **Event day:** check-in records (which day and module your team checked in for).
- **Account:** the email address you sign in with (Google sign-in or a one-time email link).

## Why we collect it

We use this information only to run Synaxis:

- to process your registration and allocate modules;
- to contact the Head Delegate (and members, for event updates) by email or WhatsApp;
- to verify payments and waivers;
- to run the event safely, including check-in and contacting a guardian in an emergency;
- to plan catering and accessibility support.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Where it is stored

- **Supabase** hosts our database and file storage. Receipts and waivers are kept in private storage and can only be opened through short-lived links by authorised organisers.
- **Resend** sends our emails (confirmations, payment updates, reminders).
- **Google** handles sign-in if you choose "Continue with Google". We receive your email address and name from Google; we never see your Google password.
- The website is hosted on **Vercel**.

These providers process data on our behalf and may store it outside Pakistan.

## Who can see it

Only members of the Cubinet, and only what their role needs:

- **Super Admins** (President and Vice President) can see all registrations.
- **Finance** organisers can see registrations, payments and waivers in order to verify them.
- **Module Heads** can see the teams in their own module.

Every change to a registration's status is recorded in an internal audit log.

## How long we keep it

We keep registration data until the event is over and results are published. We then delete or anonymise personal data within **[placeholder: 6] months** after the event, except for summary records (for example, team names and results) that we may keep for future seasons.

## Minors

Most Synaxis delegates are under 18. By registering, the Head Delegate confirms that every member's parent or guardian is aware of the registration and of this policy. A parent or guardian signs each waiver.

## Photos and media

Photos and videos are taken at Synaxis and may be used by the Heuser Science Society on social media and in reports. Registration includes consent to this. If you would prefer that a particular photo of you is not used, contact us and we will take it down where we reasonably can.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

- show you the information we hold about you or your team;
- correct anything that is wrong (Head Delegates can also edit their team until registration closes);
- delete your information, unless we still need it to run the event or to keep financial records.

Email us at the address in the website footer. We aim to reply within **[placeholder: 14] days**.

## Cookies and browser storage

We use a sign-in cookie to keep you signed in. We also store a few small settings in your browser (localStorage): your theme choice (light or dark), whether you have seen the intro animation, your registration draft (so you don't lose your answers), and which easter eggs you have found. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, email registered Head Delegates.
