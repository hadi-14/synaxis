# Terms of Service

These terms apply to everyone who registers a team for Synaxis Season 1, a science olympiad organised by students of the Heuser Science Society (HSS) at Heuser College, Karachi. By submitting a registration, the Head Delegate agrees to these terms on behalf of the whole team.

## Eligibility

- Synaxis is open to students currently in Grade 9, O-Level/Matric, AS or A-Level/Intermediate (or equivalent), and recent school graduates on a gap year, including those preparing for entrance tests like MDCAT, BizCAT or ECAT.
- University students are not eligible. A delegate who is enrolled in a university, even part-time, cannot take part.
- Teams register as a Heuser team, an official school delegation or an independent team.
- Internal (Heuser) teams must register with their college email addresses.
- We may ask for proof of enrolment.

## Teams

- A team has between **4 and 6 members, including the Head Delegate** (the exact limits are shown on the registration form).
- Only the Head Delegate fills in the registration form and is our main contact.
- Each team competes in **4 to 5 modules**. **Kabuki Caper (Criminology) is compulsory for every team.** Teams rank the other modules they want.

## Registration and payment

- Fees and deadlines are shown on the website and in your dashboard. The total is calculated when you submit, based on your team size and whether you registered before the early-bird deadline.
- Pay by bank transfer using your registration ID as the reference, then upload the receipt on your dashboard or send it to us on WhatsApp.
- Your registration is confirmed only after the organisers verify your payment. We may ask you to re-upload a receipt, or reject a payment that we cannot verify.
- Payment must be completed by the payment deadline shown on the website. Unpaid registrations may be cancelled after the deadline.
- **Refunds:** fees are non-refundable once your payment has been verified, with two exceptions: if the organisers cancel the event, every team gets a full refund; and if you pay twice or pay more than your total, we refund the duplicate or extra amount.
- **Member swaps:** instead of a refund, a team may replace a member with another eligible student until registration closes. The Head Delegate makes the change on the dashboard or by contacting us, and the new member must submit their own waiver.

## Module allocation

Modules are allocated by preference and availability once payment and waivers are complete. Your preferences are not guaranteed. The organisers' allocation is final.

## Waivers

Every delegate must submit a signed waiver of liability before the event. Teams without complete waivers may not be allocated modules or admitted on event day.

## Delegates under 18

By registering, the Head Delegate confirms that every member under 18 has their parent or guardian's permission to take part. A parent or guardian must sign the waiver for each delegate under 18.

## Code of conduct

- Treat other delegates, organisers, volunteers and Heuser College staff with respect.
- Follow all safety instructions, especially in laboratory and robotics modules.
- Cheating, plagiarism, using unauthorised devices or help, harassment and damage to property are not allowed.
- Dress neatly and appropriately for a school event on a college campus.
- Leaving the campus early requires authorisation from a chaperone or guardian.

## Disqualification

The organisers may disqualify a delegate or team, without a refund, for breaking these terms or the code of conduct, for giving false information when registering, or for behaviour that puts others at risk.

## Changes to the event

We may change the schedule, venue, modules or format if we need to. We will tell registered Head Delegates as early as we can. If the organisers cancel the event, every team receives a full refund of the fees it has paid.
